About

What it is

Capture in seconds

A note, a photo, a voice memo — tied to a child and a moment, without breaking your attention on the classroom.

Reports from real evidence

A term’s notes become a progress-report draft you read, edit and send. MonteSprout writes it with an AI service: the child’s name is swapped for a stand-in first, photos and recordings are never sent, and no copy is kept.

Works offline

Capture works with no signal at all. Your notes live on your device and sync through your own iCloud account — not a school server, and no sign-up before you start.
